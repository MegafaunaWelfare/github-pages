Our work

From a daily meal to a day in court.

Five projects, and regular drives and campaigns, cover the whole journey of care for a street animal in the Chandigarh Tricity.

Feeding Project

A meal every day for more than 1,000 animals.

Every day, our feeders take nutritious meals to more than 1,000 stray animals across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and neighbouring areas, including spots that are hard to reach.

A regular meal does more than fill a stomach. Animals that know their feeders are calmer and much easier to catch for sterilisation, vaccination and treatment, so a feeding round is often where a rescue begins.

Veterinary Aid Project

Emergency care when it matters most.

Our dedicated medical response team gives emergency treatment to injured and sick strays in and around Chandigarh.

When disaster strikes, animals are often the last to be helped. Our veterinary team has gone out to flood-hit areas, including Sultanpur Lodhi in Punjab, and we've appealed for emergency veterinary care for the goats and sheep at Bakri Mandi.

Legal Aid Project

The law protects animals, if someone uses it.

Our Legal Aid Project helps animals and the people who care for them, from filing complaints and petitions to taking legal action against cruelty. We also stand by feeders and caretakers who are harassed for looking after community animals.

Our cases include a public interest litigation in the Punjab and Haryana High Court over the takeover of Chandigarh's Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals.

Sensitisation Programmes

Changing minds, one session at a time.

Many conflicts between people and animals start with not knowing the law, or not knowing how to live alongside community animals. Our awareness sessions reach schools, colleges, residential areas and public servants.

We talk about what the law says, why sterilisation and vaccination work, how to feed responsibly, and what to do when an animal is in trouble.

Who we reach
Schools, colleges, neighbourhoods and public servants

The sanctuary

In development

A forever home, about an hour from Chandigarh.

Some animals can't safely go back to the street after treatment. We're developing an animal sanctuary about an hour from Chandigarh, where they can live out their lives with care, space and safety.

We'll share news here as it takes shape.

Drives & campaigns

Small things that save lives.

Alongside our five projects, we run regular drives across the Tricity.

Free anti-rabies vaccination

Vaccination drives that protect street animals and the people who live around them.

Reflective collars

Collars that make stray dogs visible to drivers at night and help prevent road accidents.
