About us

Standing up for animals who have no one else.

Megafauna Welfare Foundation, or MegaFauna, is a not-for-profit company in Chandigarh dedicated to the legal and ethical treatment of street animals.

Our story

Care on the street, and justice when it's needed.

MegaFauna was incorporated in June 2024 as a not-for-profit company under the Companies Act, 2013. It was co-founded by animal rights activist Udit Bhatia, one of its directors.

Our mission is to protect and improve the lives of stray and neglected animals through proactive care, legal protection and community involvement. In practice, that means a meal every day for more than 1,000 animals, emergency treatment when they're sick or hurt, and legal action when they're harmed, or when the systems meant to protect them fail.

We work alongside the feeders, volunteers and residents who look after animals in their neighbourhoods across Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula.

“Where ABC rules have been implemented properly, complaint rates and dog bite cases have sharply fallen.”

Udit Bhatia, director, speaking at the Press Club of India, as reported by ThePrint

How we work

Three ways we stand up for animals.

Proactive care

Daily meals, vaccinations and emergency treatment, so animals get help before a crisis and during one.

Legal protection

Using the law to stop cruelty, and to protect animals and the people who care for them.

Community involvement

Helping neighbourhoods understand animal welfare law and live alongside animals responsibly.

Where we work

Across the Chandigarh Tricity, and beyond when we're needed.

Our feeders and rescue team work across Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula and in neighbouring areas. When floods hit Punjab, our veterinary team went to flood-hit areas including Sultanpur Lodhi, and the sanctuary we're developing is about an hour from Chandigarh.
