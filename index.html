A stronghold of care for every paw.

About us

One company, built around the animals we care for.

Pawgarh Veterinarski Private Limited brings a family of pet-care brands together under one roof. Each brand has its own focus and personality, and all of them share the same standards of care, the same respect for animal welfare and the same belief that pet parents deserve clear, honest support.

As the parent company, Pawgarh sets the direction, protects those standards and gives every brand the backing it needs to grow and to look after more pets, better.

What's in a name?

Paw + garh

Paw
For the animals at the heart of everything we do.
Garh
Hindi for a fort: a place of safety, strength and shelter.

Together, Pawgarh is a stronghold of care for every pet.

Our brands

Pet Smile

Care that keeps tails wagging.
