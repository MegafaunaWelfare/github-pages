Who we are

Megafauna Welfare Foundation is a Chandigarh not-for-profit for the stray and neglected animals of the Tricity. We work on the street, at the vet and in court, so that no animal is left without food, care or justice.

MegaFauna was co-founded in 2024 by animal rights activist Udit Bhatia. We work alongside the feeders, volunteers and residents who look after animals in their neighbourhoods.

Who we help

Every animal on our streets counts.

Dogs are most of the animals we feed, but they aren't the only ones who need someone on their side.

  • Street dogs

    Community dogs live, eat and sleep in our sectors and markets. We feed them, get them treated, and speak up when they're hurt or taken from their areas.

    • Daily feeding rounds
    • Anti-rabies vaccination and reflective collars

  • Cats

    Street cats are easy to overlook until they're hurt. Injured and sick cats are part of our emergency veterinary work.

    • Emergency treatment
    • First-aid advice for the people who find them

  • Cows and bulls

    Stray cattle face traffic, hunger and plastic waste. Injured and sick cattle are part of our emergency veterinary work.

    • Emergency treatment
    • A future home at our sanctuary

  • Goats and sheep

    Animals at livestock markets often go without care. We've appealed for emergency veterinary care for the goats and sheep at Bakri Mandi.

    • Emergency veterinary care
    • Appeals on Give.do

  • Animals caught in floods

    When floods hit, animals are left behind. Our veterinary team has gone out to flood-hit areas, including Sultanpur Lodhi in Punjab.

    • Flood relief
    • Emergency treatment

Animal in need?

Found an injured or sick animal?

A few calm minutes can save a life. Our free app turns what you see into a report for the rescue team in under a minute, with first-aid advice while you wait.

Report it in the app What to do

  1. Keep yourself and the animal safe

    Approach slowly and quietly. A frightened or hurt animal may bite.

  2. Note the exact location

    A landmark, sector number and a photo help the team find it quickly.

  3. Water and shade, never medicine

    Many human painkillers are poisonous to animals.

Know the law

Know your rights, and theirs.

Indian law protects animals. Knowing it is the first step to standing up for them.

Read the guide

Constitution of India, Article 51A(g)

Compassion is a duty

Every citizen has a fundamental duty to have compassion for living creatures.

PCA Act, 1960 · BNS, 2023 s.325

Cruelty is a crime

Beating, torturing or causing an animal unnecessary pain is an offence. Killing, poisoning or maiming an animal is punishable with up to five years' imprisonment.

Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023

Sterilise, vaccinate, return

Community dogs are to be sterilised, vaccinated against rabies and released back in the same area they were picked up from.

This is general information, not legal advice. If you're facing a specific situation, contact us and our Legal Aid Project can guide you.
