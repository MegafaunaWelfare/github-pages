Who we are

Standing up for animals who have no one else.

Megafauna Welfare Foundation, or MegaFauna, is a not-for-profit company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 and based in Chandigarh. We are dedicated to the legal and ethical treatment of street animals.

Our mission is to protect and improve the lives of stray and neglected animals through proactive care, legal protection and community involvement. The foundation was co-founded by animal rights activist Udit Bhatia, and we work alongside the feeders, volunteers and residents who look after animals in their neighbourhoods.

Proactive care

Daily meals, vaccinations and emergency treatment, so animals get help before a crisis and during one.

Legal protection

Using the law to stop cruelty and to protect animals and the people who care for them.

Community involvement

Helping neighbourhoods understand animal welfare law and live alongside animals responsibly.

Our work

Four programmes. One goal: no animal left behind.

From a daily meal to a day in court, our programmes cover the whole journey of care for a street animal.

Feeding Project

Nutritious meals, every single day, for more than 1,000 stray animals across the Chandigarh Tricity and neighbouring areas.

Veterinary Aid Project

Emergency treatment for injured and sick strays, delivered by a dedicated medical response team operating in and around Chandigarh.

Legal Aid Project

Legal assistance to protect animals and their caretakers, from filing complaints and petitions to taking legal action against cruelty.

Sensitisation Programmes

Awareness and education sessions that help communities understand animal welfare laws and coexist responsibly with animals.

Drives & campaigns

  • Free anti-rabies vaccination

    Vaccination drives that protect street animals and the people who live around them.

  • Reflective collars

    Collars that make stray dogs visible to drivers at night and help prevent road accidents.

  • Cruelty case support

    Guidance and follow-through for people who witness or report cruelty anywhere in Chandigarh.

Animal in need?

Found an injured or sick animal? Here's what to do.

A few calm minutes can save a life. Follow these steps, then get in touch so our team can help.

Contact our team

  1. Keep yourself and the animal safe

    Approach slowly and quietly. A frightened or hurt animal may bite. If it's near traffic, alert drivers and keep a safe distance.

  2. Note the exact location

    A landmark, sector number and a photo or short video help the rescue team find the animal quickly and judge what it needs.

  3. Contact us or a nearby vet

    Share the location and photos. Tell us whether the animal can move, is bleeding, or seems to be in pain.

  4. Offer water and shade, never medicine

    Don't give human medicines. Many common painkillers are poisonous to animals. Water and shade are safe ways to help.

  5. Stay nearby if you can

    Keep an eye on the animal until help arrives so it doesn't wander off or get lost.

Know the law

Know your rights, and theirs.

Indian law protects animals. Knowing it is the first step to standing up for them.

Constitution of India, Article 51A(g)

Compassion is a duty

Every citizen has a fundamental duty to have compassion for living creatures.

Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 · BNS, 2023 s.325

Cruelty is a crime

Beating, torturing or causing an animal unnecessary pain is an offence. Killing, poisoning or maiming an animal is punishable with up to five years' imprisonment.

Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023

Sterilise, vaccinate, return

Community dogs are to be sterilised, vaccinated against rabies and released back in the same area they were picked up from.

Good practice for feeders

Feed with care

Use a designated or agreed spot away from play areas and entrances, feed at regular times and clean up afterwards. If you're harassed for feeding, talk to us.

This is general information, not legal advice. Laws, rules and court orders change and can apply differently from place to place. If you're facing a specific situation, contact us and our Legal Aid Project can guide you.
