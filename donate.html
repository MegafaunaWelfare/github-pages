Questions

About giving.

Anything else you'd like to know? Ask us.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Donations made through Give.do are eligible for a tax deduction under section 80G of the Income Tax Act. Keep your donation receipt for your records.

What does my donation pay for?

Our daily work: food for the animals we feed, medicines and treatment, anti-rabies vaccines, reflective collars and the costs of legal action against cruelty. Donations also help us build the sanctuary.

Can I give food, medicines or supplies instead?

Yes. Get in touch and we'll tell you what's needed most right now.

Who runs Megafauna Welfare Foundation?

MegaFauna is a not-for-profit company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U85500PB2024NPL061864), co-founded in 2024 by animal rights activist Udit Bhatia. More about us.

Can I help without giving money?

Absolutely. You can volunteer, intern with us, invite us to speak, or report cruelty. See all the ways to get involved.
